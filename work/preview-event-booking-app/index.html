PreView event booking app

A mobile app that makes discovering, scheduling and managing events simple for attendees and organisers.

Discipline
UI/UX design
When
Sep – Oct 2024
Tools
Figma
Open the Figma prototype

The challenge

Finding events, booking them and keeping track of plans usually means juggling several apps. Organisers have the same problem from the other side.

My approach

A clean, modern interface that puts navigation first and follows accessibility best practice, so the app works for as many people as possible.

Process

  1. Designed the onboarding, discovery and booking screens in Figma.
  2. Built an interactive prototype to walk through the full flow.

The solution

An intuitive platform where users can manage events, stay informed and take part in event planning.
