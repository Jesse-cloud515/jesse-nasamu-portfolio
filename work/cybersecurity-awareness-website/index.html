Cybersecurity awareness website

A website and mobile design that helps ordinary users recognise online threats and get help.

Discipline
UI/UX design · Cybersecurity
Tools
Figma
Open the Figma file

The challenge

Security advice is often written for specialists. This site needed to speak to everyday users about online theft and ransomware, and point them to services when something goes wrong.

My approach

I combined my cybersecurity training with UI design: plain headlines, strong contrast and a clear path from awareness content to services such as penetration testing and incident response.

Process

  1. Planned content around common threats, including online theft and ransomware.
  2. Designed desktop and mobile layouts in Figma.
  3. Added a services area and a community forum for questions.

The solution

Responsive designs covering the homepage, threat articles, services, a password generator tool and a forum.
