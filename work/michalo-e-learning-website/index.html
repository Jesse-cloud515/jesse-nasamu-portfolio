Michalo e-learning website

A learning platform designed so students can find a course, start it and keep going without friction.

Discipline
UI/UX design
When
Nov – Dec 2024
Tools
Figma
Open the Figma prototype

The challenge

Online learning only works if people stay with it. The aim was to make online education intuitive and enjoyable, with a smooth journey from the first visit to finishing a course.

My approach

I designed around the learner: easy navigation, a personalised learning experience and consistent, accessible interactions across the platform.

Process

  1. Designed user-centred interfaces in Figma with a focus on ease of navigation.
  2. Shaped the experience around personalised learning.
  3. Prioritised consistency and accessibility across every screen.

The solution

A desktop website with a clear welcome, course discovery and sign-up flow, delivered as an interactive Figma prototype.
